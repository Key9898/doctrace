1. INTRODUCTION This Business Requirement Document ("BRD") is prepared to define the business objectives, operational requirements, strategic direction, and high-level functional expectations for the development of the EZ Audit and Assurance Intelligence ("EZAAI") platform. EZAAI is intended to become an AI-powered audit and assurance operating platform designed to modernize professional audit workflows, evidence management, financial reporting processes, reviewer collaboration, and compliance operations for audit firms and financial professionals. The BRD serves as a foundational reference document for: product planning, software development, UI/UX design, audit workflow structuring, AI integration planning, system architecture development, and stakeholder alignment. This document is intended to support communication and coordination among: product owners, software developers, audit methodology advisors, implementation teams, management stakeholders, and future operational users. The BRD will continue to evolve throughout the product development lifecycle and may be updated periodically based on operational feedback, business requirements, regulatory developments, and technology enhancements. 2. BUSINESS BACKGROUND The audit and assurance industry continues to experience increasing operational complexity due to: growing regulatory expectations, increasing documentation requirements, expanding financial reporting standards, heightened reviewer scrutiny, and rising client expectations for efficiency and responsiveness. Many audit firms, especially within emerging and developing markets, continue to rely heavily on: manual spreadsheets, disconnected document storage, fragmented review workflows, paper-based evidence management, and non-integrated communication processes. These operational limitations often result in: inefficient audit execution, duplicated work, review bottlenecks, inconsistent documentation quality, delayed engagement completion, and limited visibility over audit progress and evidence status. At the same time, global audit software solutions are often: expensive, overly complex, insufficiently localized, difficult for SME firms to adopt, or not fully aligned with practical audit workflows in emerging markets. Additionally, many existing platforms focus primarily on digital document storage rather than intelligent audit workflow management and AI-assisted audit reasoning. EZAAI is being developed to address these operational gaps by combining: professional audit methodology, intelligent workflow management, AI-assisted operational support, reviewer-centric design, evidence intelligence, and scalable cloud technology within a unified assurance platform. 3. PROJECT PURPOSE The purpose of EZAAI is to create a scalable and intelligent audit operating platform capable of improving: audit quality, workflow efficiency, reviewer visibility, evidence traceability, and professional collaboration. The platform is intended to simplify complex audit processes while maintaining alignment with applicable professional standards and operational best practices. EZAAI is designed to support: external audit engagements, assurance workflows, financial reporting activities, evidence management, audit documentation, and professional review processes. The platform will initially focus on supporting: audit firms, accounting firms, SME and MNC audit environments, and professional assurance teams within Myanmar and selected ASEAN markets. Future expansion may include broader support for: internal audit, tax compliance, ESG reporting, governance workflows, and enterprise compliance management. 4. PRODUCT VISION The long-term vision of EZAAI is: "To become a leading AI-powered audit and assurance operating platform for emerging and mid-tier professional markets across ASEAN and future international markets." The platform aims to transform traditional audit operations into intelligent, evidence-driven, and reviewer-focused workflows that improve operational consistency and professional productivity. EZAAI is intended to evolve beyond a traditional audit software application into a broader assurance intelligence ecosystem capable of supporting multiple professional service functions. 5. PRODUCT POSITIONING EZAAI is positioned as: "An AI-Powered Audit and Assurance Intelligence Platform" The platform is not intended to replicate existing software products directly. Instead, it is designed to combine: practical audit methodology, intelligent workflow automation, AI-assisted reasoning, modern SaaS architecture, and reviewer-centric operational design. The platform will focus on balancing: professional compliance, operational simplicity, affordability, scalability, and intelligent automation. The core product philosophy emphasizes: speed, simplicity, reviewer efficiency, evidence intelligence, and human-controlled AI assistance. 6. USER ACCESS GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES The EZAAI permission structure shall follow the following core governance principles: 2.1 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) User access shall be governed primarily through predefined role-based permissions. Each user shall only have access to: authorized engagements, assigned operational functions, and approved workflow responsibilities. 2.2 Least Privilege Principle Users shall receive only the minimum level of access necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities. Operational access should be restricted to reduce: unauthorized changes, accidental deletion, and inappropriate visibility of sensitive information. 2.3 Segregation of Duties The platform shall support operational segregation among: preparers, reviewers, approvers, and administrative users. Critical workflow actions such as: engagement finalization, file locking, and archive export shall remain restricted to authorized senior personnel. 2.4 Multi-Tenant Data Isolation The platform shall maintain strict data separation between firms operating within the multi-tenant SaaS environment. Users shall not have visibility over: other firms, unrelated engagements, or unauthorized organizational data. 2.5 Controlled Administrative Oversight Administrative users may retain technical and operational oversight authority; however, such access shall remain governed through: audit logs, access monitoring, and restricted operational policies. 3.
